The EMC method

We don’t guess. We trace the chain.

Small-business digital problems often live between vendors: registrar, host, email provider, website builder, form plugin, booking tool, payment link, or phone system. Everyone points somewhere else. EMC2 Digital follows the actual path until the weak link is found.

Start with the chain
1

Map

Identify the domain, host, email platform, website, forms, admins, and recovery paths.

2

Diagnose

Test the real customer path and isolate what is misconfigured, missing, expired, blocked, or fragile.

3

Fix or route

Some issues can be fixed directly. Others need a host, platform vendor, web developer, or specialist. The first job is to identify the right path.

4

Verify

Confirm the result with customer-view testing instead of assuming “it should work now.”
