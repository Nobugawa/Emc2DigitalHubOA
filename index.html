The EMC method

We don’t guess. We trace the chain.

Small-business digital problems usually live between vendors: domain registrar, host, email provider, website builder, CRM, form plugin, payment tool, and phone system. Everyone points somewhere else. EMC2 Digital follows the actual path until the failure is found.
