Plain-English digital guide

Your website can be “up” and still not be working

Many monitoring tools only check whether a page loads. That is useful, but it does not prove your business website is doing its job.

What uptime misses

The homepage may load while the contact form fails, mobile layout breaks, SSL warnings appear, booking links disconnect, payment links expire, or the wrong phone number is displayed.

The customer path matters

A business website is not just a brochure. A visitor needs to find the right information, trust the page, take action, submit a form or call, and know what happens next.

Mobile and browser differences

Something can work on your desktop and fail on a customer’s phone. Cached pages, browser warnings, device-specific layouts, and plugin conflicts can hide problems from the owner.

The right kind of test

Test like a customer: open the site on mobile, click the main calls to action, submit the form, check the inbox, try the booking link, test key pages, and verify that the site feels trustworthy.

The business point

A website should not only exist. It should help customers trust you, reach you, and become revenue. Anything less is an invisible leak.
