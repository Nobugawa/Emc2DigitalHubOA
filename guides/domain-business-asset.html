Plain-English digital guide

Your domain name is a business asset — treat it like one

A domain is not just a web address. It controls your website, email, trust, search visibility, ads, reputation, and sometimes your entire customer path.

What your domain controls

Your domain can point to your website, authenticate your email, route subdomains, support ads and analytics, and help customers trust that they are dealing with the right business.

The ownership question

The most important question is simple: who owns and controls the registrar account? If the answer is a web designer, former employee, old email account, or “I am not sure,” the business has a continuity risk.

DNS is the control panel

DNS records tell the internet where your website lives, who can send your email, how services verify your business, and how subdomains behave. Bad records can break email, SSL, websites, tracking, and verification.

Renewal and lock settings matter

Auto-renewal, payment method, transfer lock, domain privacy, and recovery email should be deliberate. A domain expiration can be much more damaging than a normal website outage.

The practical rule

The business should control the domain, understand who has access, and document enough information to recover or transfer it if the current helper disappears.
